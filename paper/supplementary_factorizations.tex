\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Key findings:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item 79 of 107 operations (74\%) are fully realizable on analog hardware using only P1--P12.
\item All 22 GPU-mappable operations are blocked by X2 (dynamic topology) or X4 (symbolic tracing), both available on modern GPUs.
\item Only 6 operations (5.6\%) are fundamentally unmappable: Speculative Decoding (X1+X3), MCTS (X2+X3), Beam Search (X2+X3), CTC Loss (X2+X3), WL-Test (X1), BPE Tokenizer (X1).
\item The most frequently invoked primes are P1 ($\Sigma$, 89 appearances) and P11 ($\times$, 62 appearances) --- accumulation and variable multiplication are the backbone of neural computation.
\item The minimal factorization is Temperature Scaling (P2$\cdot$P12, 2 primes). The maximal is Nucleus Sampling (7 primes).
\end{itemize}
